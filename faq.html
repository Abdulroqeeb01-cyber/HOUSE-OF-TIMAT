Frequently asked questions.

A few helpful answers about shopping with HOUSE-OF-TIMAT.

How do I place an order?

Browse the collection, add your favourite pieces to your bag and continue to checkout. Our current website is a demo; live checkout will be enabled when payment processing is connected.

How do I know which size to choose?

Please check the size guide on the product listing when available. If you need help choosing a fit, contact our team before ordering.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timelines depend on your location and will be confirmed when your order is placed. Contact us for current delivery estimates.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Return and exchange eligibility should be confirmed with our team before purchase. Items should be unworn and in their original condition.

How can I check product availability?

Product availability is shown on each product. If an item is marked sold out, please contact us to ask about a restock.

Do you accept custom orders?

This is a ready-to-wear collection. Contact us to ask whether a particular adjustment or request can be accommodated.
